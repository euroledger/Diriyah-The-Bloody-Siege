SUPPLY LINE BATTLE DRM
For each two locations occupied by the Ottomans, the Wahhabis receive one right column shift on the Supply Line Battle table

Ottomans control Mawiyya -> 1 shift right
Ottomans control Unaizah/Buraidah -> 2 shift right
Ottomans control Shaqra -> 3 shift right

BATTLES
Each side rolls a die on the Battle Table, using the column relating to their total strengh (in Strength Points). Result is number of enemy SPs lost. Apply hits simulataneously.
